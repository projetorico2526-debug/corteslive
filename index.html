About CortesLive Studio

CortesLive Studio is a Windows desktop app for live streamers. It analyzes the streamer's own live stream on his or her computer and creates short vertical clips with captions. Optionally, the user can connect his or her TikTok account and YouTube channel (Sign in with Google) and upload a chosen clip as a TikTok video or a YouTube Short to his or her own account. The app only uses the YouTube Data API to upload the clip the user selects (youtube.upload) and to show the connected channel name (youtube.readonly).
