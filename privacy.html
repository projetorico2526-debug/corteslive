Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What the app accesses

TikTok. Through TikTok's official Login Kit, and only with the user's permission, the app receives an access token to:

  • user.info.basic: read basic profile information (display name and avatar), shown on the posting screen;
  • video.upload and video.publish: upload the clips the user chooses, as a draft or as a direct post.

YouTube. CortesLive Studio uses the YouTube API Services. When the user clicks “Conectar YouTube” and signs in with Google, the app asks for:

  • youtube.upload: upload, as a YouTube Short on the user's own channel, only the clip the user selects, with the title, description and privacy the user reviewed (videos.insert);
  • youtube.readonly: read the name and ID of the user's own channel (channels.list, mine=true) to show which channel is connected.

The app does not read, download, analyze, edit or delete any other YouTube videos, comments, playlists, analytics or data. By using the YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where data is stored

The access tokens (TikTok and YouTube) stay on the user's own computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), and can only be read by the user's Windows account. They are never sent to us. Videos go directly from the user's computer to TikTok or YouTube.

We run a small server (Cloudflare Worker) only to: (1) exchange the login code for tokens, because the OAuth client secret cannot be inside the app (the tokens pass through and are not stored); (2) check the license key; (3) receive support messages the user chooses to send. For license delivery we keep the buyer's name, e-mail and purchase code received from Hotmart. We do not store any YouTube or TikTok data, and we do not sell or share data with third parties.

Video processing

The analysis of the live streams (speech transcription and choosing the best moments) runs locally on the user's computer. No video is sent anywhere except to TikTok or YouTube, and only when the user clicks the post/upload button.

Deleting your data and revoking access

Click “Desconectar” (Disconnect) next to TikTok or YouTube in the app's “Postar” tab: this revokes the access and deletes the saved tokens from the computer. You can also revoke CortesLive Studio's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings › Third-party access, and in TikTok under Settings › Security › Apps and services. To delete license or support data we keep, e-mail us and we delete it within 30 days.

Google user data: what we collect, how we use it and Limited Use

Data accessed from Google: an OAuth access token and refresh token, the user's YouTube channel ID and channel name. Use: only to (1) show the user which channel is connected and (2) upload, at the user's request, the clip the user selected. Storage: only on the user's own computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI; we keep no copy on any server. Sharing: never shared, sold or transferred to anyone, and never used for advertising, profiling, credit or AI/ML model training. Retention: until the user clicks “Desconectar” or revokes access in the Google account, when the tokens are deleted.

CortesLive Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information we collect when you buy

When you buy on Hotmart we receive your name, e-mail and the purchase code, used only to create and deliver your license key, to activate it on up to 2 computers (we store an anonymous ID of each computer, a hash) and to deactivate it after a refund. Payment data is handled only by Hotmart; we never see card numbers. If you send a support message, we store the message, the contact you typed and the app log, for up to 180 days.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Tokens are encrypted on your computer. License keys are signed (Ed25519) and verified offline. Access to our server is restricted to the developer.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the “Last updated” date when this policy changes. Important changes are also shown in the app's update notes.

Política de Privacidade

Pelo login oficial do TikTok e do Google, e só com a sua autorização, o programa recebe um acesso para mostrar o nome da sua conta/canal e enviar os cortes que você escolher (no YouTube, como Shorts no seu próprio canal). O CortesLive usa os Serviços de API do YouTube: ao usar essa função, você concorda com os Termos de Serviço do YouTube, e a Política de Privacidade do Google se aplica. Os acessos ficam no seu computador, criptografados pelo Windows; nosso servidor só faz a troca do código de login (sem guardar nada), confere a licença e recebe mensagens de suporte. Guardamos nome, e-mail e código da compra vindos da Hotmart para entregar a chave. Não vendemos nem compartilhamos dados. A análise das lives roda no seu PC, e os vídeos só saem quando você clica para postar. Para apagar os dados, clique em “Desconectar” no programa, revogue o acesso em security.google.com/settings/security/permissions ou no TikTok, e peça por e-mail a exclusão dos dados de compra/suporte.
