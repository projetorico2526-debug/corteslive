Terms of Service

Last updated: November 23, 2026

CortesLive Studio ("the app") is a Windows desktop application that creates short vertical clips from the user's own live streams and lets the user post those clips to the user's own TikTok account and YouTube channel (as Shorts).

1. Use of the app

The app may only be used with videos the user owns or has the right to publish.

2. TikTok account

The connection to TikTok uses TikTok's official Login Kit. No video is posted unless the user selects the clip, reviews the caption and settings, and clicks “Post”.

2a. YouTube account

The connection to YouTube uses Google's official sign-in (OAuth) and the YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy. No video is uploaded unless the user selects the clip, reviews the title, description and privacy, and clicks the upload button. Access can be revoked at any time in the app or at Google security settings.

3. Content

The user is solely responsible for the content posted and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Limits and availability

Posting limits are set by TikTok. The app is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind.

5. Disconnecting

The user can disconnect the TikTok account at any time with the “Disconnect” button in the app, or in TikTok under Settings › Security › Apps and services.
